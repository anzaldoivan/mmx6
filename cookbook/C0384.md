# An Exec bp on a dispatch jalr hides the entry bp at its overlay target

Problem: PCSX-Redux runs with -interpreter -debugger. An Exec bp sat on the front-end dispatch `jalr v1` at 0x8001D178. The entry bp on its overlay target 0x800EA5C8 then counted 0 hits (rock_18, D_800710E8[8], options). With the jalr bp disabled it counted 911 hits, the state-8 dispatch count, ra 0x8001D180. SLUS table targets fired through the same jalr. The overlay target did not (2.6 T25.c1).

Move (tools/mmx6/redux/boot.lua, T25.c1):
- Count entries reached through a known dispatch jalr in the jalr hook itself. The target is the jump register: v1 at 0x8001D178, a1 at 0x800350F0. Record the first frame, and ra = jalr pc + 8.
- In the entry-bp hook, skip a hit whose ra == jalr pc + 8. Each entry then counts once, whichever bp fires.
- A range Exec bp over the overlay still sees the instructions run. Use it as the control (C0376).

Trap: 0 hits on a dispatch target under a jalr bp do not prove "not reached". Check the range bp's pc span, or the state count for that target.
