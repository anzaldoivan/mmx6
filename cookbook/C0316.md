# Field-watch control on a small leaf: Exec bp at entry and on its `jr ra`, the delay-slot store read from the register

Problem: prove a runtime field watcher reads the right offsets before trusting what it observes about unknown fields.

Move:
- Pick a gate-proven leaf whose body is an identity over the fields (mmx6: move_object 0x8002D2B0, `x += x_vel` and `y -= y_vel`, src/SLUS_013.95/object/object.c).
- Exec breakpoint at entry: record a0 and the input words. Exec breakpoint on the `jr ra` (find it in the retail bytes): re-read and check the identity.
- Trap: the store in the `jr ra` delay slot (`sw v1,0xC(a0)`) has not happened at that breakpoint. Check that half against the register (v1), not memory.
- A control needs both halves exercised with nonzero inputs. Log which half had a nonzero value (intro_stage: every vy was 0).
- Summary `FIELDS CONTROL <pass>/<n>`, rc 1 on any fail or n < 3.

Example: tools/mmx6/redux/fields.lua (T5.c1, 1330/1330 on intro_stage).
