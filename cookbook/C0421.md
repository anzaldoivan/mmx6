# Refit a shard cost model on one timed shard after a lever, and cap the group size at the calibration

- Move (3.0 T6): after the T4 red-R5 bisect, one timed shard took 1662 s. The old constants estimated 7644 s.
- Its GROUP lines gave the refit. SLUS: 101 units in 1622 s (16.1 s/unit). Rock, least squares over 48 groups: 14.1 s grouped, 29.5 s isolated.
- Each rate is the measured rate +20%, rounded up.
- Cap: per-unit cost grows with group size (C0414). A cheaper rate would pack 3-4x more SLUS units per shard, which extrapolates the fit.
- So thshard.py SLUS_MAX, the calibrated group size, closes a shard. A self-test plant proves that the cap alone splits.
- Fit input: `PROPAGATE GROUP <prog> units u isolated i fallback f reisolated r r5 k wall Ns`, with isolated = i + r.
- Cost: the old estimate bounds one shard, under the 3 h cap. It must run alone, with no fleet on the shared 8 cores.
