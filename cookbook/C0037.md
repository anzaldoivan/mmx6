# C0037 — cpp 12 -nostdinc drops the stdc-predef.h pre-include that old gcc 2.x cc1 warns on
tags: cpp,old-gcc,cc1,psx · date: 2026-10-01 · phase/task: 1.4/T2 · origin: mmx6 T2

Modern cpp pre-includes stdc-predef.h. The line markers of that file make gcc 2.x cc1 warn `unrecognized text at end of #line`. The `-nostdinc` flag removes the pre-include. You do not need `-P`, so the line markers stay and the cc1 errors keep file:line.

Source: phase 1.4 T1 finding, T2 fix.
