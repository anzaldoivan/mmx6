# C0055 — Dup census: load-bearing-mask control (twin must split unmasked)
tags: census,duplicates,control · date: 2026-10-01 · phase/task: 1.5/T6 · origin: T6 census

A duplicate census needs a load-bearing-mask control. With relocation masking off, the same planted twin must split into two classes. Otherwise, a mask that never applies still passes the twin control.
