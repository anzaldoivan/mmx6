# C0434 — Declaration unify: go toward the specific type, since a loose shared body breaks other includers
tags: declsync,declunify,prototype,extern,M2C_UNK,xkey,r2,unify · date: 2026-11-10 · phase/task: 3.1/T3 · origin: mmx6 T3.c1

# Unifying a unit toward a less-typed shared body breaks the other includers
- Symptom: R2 `conflicting types for X`, and the shared body declares X loosely (`extern M2C_UNK g_Player;`, `void animate_object(T*)`). The unit and other shared bodies declare it from a header (`extern struct X4_PlayerObj g_Player;`, `s32 animate_object();` in pilot_globals.h).
- Mechanism: rewriting the unit-side declarations to the body's spelling also rewrites shared bodies that other units include. Those units take the specific type from the header, so they stop compiling (4228C.c.o, rock_09/E9860.c.o), and bank.xkey_guard refuses the edit.
- Lever: unify toward the more specific declaration. When the body is the loose side, change the body's declaration line, never its code, and check that the exemplar's includers keep their bytes. Edit the unit side only when the body is the specific side.
- Case: 3.1 T3.c1 pilot, rock_15 11 g_Player rows and animate_object/func_8002F288/func_800E9E88 rows. The unit-side direction gated 1 of 23 rows (rock_15:0x800EA4D4).
