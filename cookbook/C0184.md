# One-set constant store: write the constant directly, not through a shared local

Case: W19 rock_07:0x800EB114 (113/117 → 117/117, attempt 2; logs/T19.c53.md).
- Symptom: L07 residual; in two switch arms the second call-argument constant is scheduled after a byte constant store; combine folds any early arg local back into the call site.
- Lever: drop the shared s32 local that held the byte constant and store the literal per arm (`B(p, 0x60) = 0x..;`). A pseudo set once gets sched1's priority boost: its load is placed next to its store and the arg loads go first.
- Second half: move the neighbouring zero store (`B(p, 0x5C) = 0;`) up right after it, ahead of the symbol store; without it local-alloc puts the constant in the second return register and sched2 sinks the store (103/117).
- Did not work: a basic-block boundary between the arg load and the call (the address-taken label is deleted before combine).
