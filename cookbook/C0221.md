# Recovery and reconcile passes: re-declare what earlier steps dropped
- A recovery step that rewrites declarations must re-declare whatever an earlier ladder step dropped. For example, a cast can drop a conflicting prototype and leave the standalone probe undeclared. The step must also carry the prelude macros that its TU spelling uses.
- A regate that respells callee declarations can place a macro before its #define. Then the TU build passes and the standalone compile fails. Scan for use-before-define after any declaration rewrite.
- Reconcile names one refused caller cast per gate run. Grep every cast of the callee across the TUs of the program, and fix all mismatched casts in one regate.
