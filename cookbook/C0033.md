# All-asm overlays: data words through gas need -march=r4000

One-TU all-asm overlays push data words through gas as instructions; rabbitizer decodes some as MIPS II/III, which `-march=r3000` rejects. Assemble with `-march=r4000 -mtune=r3000` and let the hash gate prove bytes unchanged.
