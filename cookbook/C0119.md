# C0119 — Assign an s16 to an s32 local before negating into a halfword to keep the sign extension
tags: lever,sign-extension,negate,halfword,s16,local · date: 2026-10-04 · phase/task: 1.8/T13 · origin: mmx6 W4

1. **Residual (tell)**: retail sign-extends a halfword (`sll/sra 16`) before `negu` and the `sh`; the draft omits the extension.
2. **Mechanism**: observed. gcc 2.95 drops the extension when the negated s16 goes straight back to a halfword store (only the low 16 bits matter); an s32 intermediate forces the widening.
3. **Lever**: `s32 t = obj->s16field; obj->other = -t;` instead of `obj->other = -obj->s16field;`.
4. **Byte proof**: wave W4 (1.8/T13 c5), banked.
