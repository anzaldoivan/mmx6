# C0168 — Drop a twin's pin on a zero-initialised flag: its known zero feeds the loop counter
tags: lever,pin,twin,cse,regalloc · date: 2026-10-09 · phase/task: 1.8.1/T14 · origin: mmx6 W14 rock_20 EBB30

# A twin's register pin on a zero-initialised flag can be the residual: drop it

Symptom: a body seeded from a banked twin stalls a few rows short with a register shift around a loop counter init.

Cause: a numerically pinned flag set to 0 makes its known-zero register reusable. Then cse initialises the loop counter from it, and that shifts the registers of the block.

Lever: remove the pin from the flag (keep the twin's other pins). The compiler then materialises the constant zero fresh.

Evidence: W14 rock_20 EBB30 (140 → 155/155). The seed twin is rock_20 EB2D4.
Related: C0141 (numeric pins on constants), C0103.
