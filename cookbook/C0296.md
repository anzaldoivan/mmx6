# C0296 — TU boundary moving a compiled 8-aligned jtbl off retail alignment is red

# A TU boundary that shifts a compiled 8-aligned jump table off retail alignment is red by construction

Context: carving a c unit (carve.py opt) whose head keeps a function with a gcc-compiled jump table.
- gcc emits the table in .rodata aligned 8. Its offset inside the unit's .rodata depends on what precedes it (asm jtbls of earlier members).
- Retail address of the table mod 8 fixes which unit-rodata starts are possible.
- Example (2.4 T23, rock_04): E98F4 ≡ 4 mod 8 fits only a unit rodata start ≡ 4 mod 8 (E9864).
- A split at EBAA0 put the table after a 0x34 asm jtbl. The result: 4-byte pad, bin +4 bytes, sha red.
- Predict before carving: (table retail addr - new unit rodata start) must keep the table's 8-alignment with no pad. If not, the boundary contradicts retail. Pick another split.
- `carve.py opt --gate` reverts such a carve automatically (CARVE REFUSE carve-red).
Related: C0226 (carved jtbl overruns its bank by an alignment pad).
