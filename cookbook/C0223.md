# Rung scheduling: the largest group is the wall-time floor
- The wall time of a grouped rung is at least its largest program group. Before you project, split that group across idle trees, or sample its rate under full concurrency.
- A per-unit rate sampled early in a group under-projects when per-unit cost grows with a whole-binary rebuild per unit.
- Run the self-test rungs (or the cheap self-test rung alone) before the long data rung. Then a self-test failure does not surface after a 20-hour rung.
