# C0044 — maspsx aspsx-version rungs >= 2.60 are byte-identical under -G0 with no $gp access
tags: maspsx,aspsx,pin,ladder,psx · date: 2026-10-01 · phase/task: 1.4/T6 · origin: mmx6 T6

In maspsx.py the only version-dependent behaviours above 2.50 are `sltu_at` (< 2.60: `sltu rd,rs,<negative imm>` via $at) and gp handling (>= 2.70, >= 2.80). Under `-G0` with no gp-relative access, 2.60-2.86 are byte-identical (2.56 too unless the code has a negative-immediate sltiu). A ladder listing them separately can never reach a unique pin; collapse to one representative (rule G104).
