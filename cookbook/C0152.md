# C0152 — A divide by a constant compiled as a real divide: put the divisor in a local, the second one late
tags: lever,divide,constant,local,reciprocal,trap · date: 2026-10-06 · phase/task: 1.8.1/T10 · origin: mmx6 W10 SLUS 4BB5C

Symptom: retail divides by a constant with a real runtime divide (plus its divide-by-zero trap) where cc1 folds a literal `/ 10` into a reciprocal multiply; the draft is long by the multiply sequence and misaligned after it.

Lever: hold the divisor in a local assigned the constant (`s32 d = 10; q = v / d;`), one local per block that divides. With two divisors in one function, use two locals and assign the second one late, just before its use (after any other use of the same constant), so the trap's freshly reloaded divisor lands where retail has it.

Case: W10 SLUS_013.95:0x8004BB5C (T10.c8, 56 → 61 then match), rock_04:0x800F2CC4 (T10.c17, two divisors den/den2, 64 → match).
Related: C0110 (remainder spelling), C0135 (statement order).
