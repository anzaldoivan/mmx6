# C0011 — ISO9660 dir-record XA attribute is big-endian. Form 2 = 0x1000, cross-check submode bit 5

# XA attribute in the ISO9660 directory record is big-endian

The CD-XA system-use field of an ISO9660 directory record holds a 16-bit attribute word. The record stores this
word big-endian, unlike the both-endian ISO fields around it. Bit `0x1000` means Form 2 (mode 2 form 2: XA audio,
STR video). Bit `0x2000` means interleaved.

Cross-check: in the subheader of the first sector of the file, bit 5 (`0x20`) of the submode byte means Form 2.
Both must agree. If they do not agree, treat this as an error.
