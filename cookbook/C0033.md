# All-asm overlays: data words through gas need -march=r4000

One-TU all-asm overlays push data words through gas as instructions. rabbitizer decodes some words as MIPS II/III, and `-march=r3000` rejects those instructions. Assemble with `-march=r4000 -mtune=r3000`. Let the hash gate prove that the bytes are unchanged.
