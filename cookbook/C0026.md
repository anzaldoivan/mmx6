# C0026 — Ghidra may emit switchdataD_ labels every 8 B inside one switch table. Merge the labels within a bounded table
tags: ghidra,switch,jump-table,labels,boundaries · date: 2026-10-01 · phase/task: 1.2/T8 · origin: mmx6 T8

Ghidra can label one large jump table with a `switchdataD_` label every 8 bytes. When you count tables, bound each table by the `sltiu` limit of its dispatch. Treat the labels inside that byte range as one table. The `Address table[n]` count of Ghidra is advisory and can overrun into the next table.
