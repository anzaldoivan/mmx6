# C0078 — lw+andi 0xff vs lbu: combine merges the mask only if the word dies
tags: idiom,sym-narrow-load,G-combine,combine,lbu,mask · date: 2026-10-01 · phase/task: 1.7/T4 · origin: mmx6 repro

1. **Residual (tell)**: `lw` + `andi rX,rY,0xff` where the target has one `lbu` (or the reverse). `sym-narrow-load`
2. **Mechanism**: combine (G-combine). An AND with a 2^n-1 mask becomes a zero-extending extraction, so `(and (mem:SI) 255)` turns into `(zero_extend:SI (mem:QI))` = `lbu`; but if the loaded word is still live after the AND, try_combine must keep the load's set too, and the merge fails.
   - src:gcc-2.95.2/gcc/combine.c:6037 "else if ((i = exact_log2 (INTVAL (XEXP (x, 1)) + 1)) >= 0)"
   - src:gcc-2.95.2/gcc/combine.c:1600 "added_sets_2 = ! dead_or_set_p (i3, i2dest);"
   - a-side `.combine`: `(zero_extend:SI (mem:QI (reg:SI 4 a0) 0)))`.
3. **Lever**: a masked word with no other use folds into `lbu`; any second use of the full word (even a second `*p` that cse merges) keeps `lw` + `andi`. To get `lbu` back, drop or re-source the other use. Inertness checked: b differs (+2 instructions).
