# C0329 — Hoisting a C0117 neighbour extern strips a conflict's view tag
tags: extern,header,hoist,globtypes,view,C0117 · date: 2026-11-02 · phase/task: 2.5/T10 · origin: mmx6 T10.c1

## Problem
Hoisting duplicated externs into a header raises globtypes' non-view conflict count.
## Mechanism
globtypes tags a conflict `view` (C0117, one extern per field of a splat blob) only on one condition.
The file must also declare a neighbour symbol within 0x10 above the conflict key.
Removing that neighbour's extern (now in the header) strips the tag.
The conflict then counts as non-view (T10.c1: 4 -> 8).
## Fix
Before removing a hoisted extern from a file, check the file's conflict keys.
Keep any extern within 0x10 above one. The header still declares it, and the duplicate is harmless.
Re-run `globtypes.py --all` after a build.
