# Near-member chains: re-head per bank, draft the head with its members
- A near (non-exact) member re-heads onto the next cluster member each time its head banks. A chain shrinks by about one wave per head, so expect a remainder wave of 1-2 cards.
- Each leverage wave surfaces the near members of its banked heads as the next undrawn layer. The remainder shrinks geometrically, not to zero in one wave.
- Draft a chain head together with all its member-of rows in one wave. Members seeded from the draft of the head closed a 7-of-7 chain in one wave.
- A near member whose head differs in shape is drafted faster from a same-TU sibling with the same call pattern than from the head body.
- A lever that closes a near member can leave its plateau head unchanged. Retest the head, especially when a branch sits ahead of the stores.
- A draw-exclude hold on a member row is invisible while its head is undrawn. Audit the holds for every row that a wave stopped or failed.
