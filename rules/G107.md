# G107 — Sibling-game C is measured and adopted under this binary's pin
id: G107 · group: G · status: active · tags: decomp,prior-art,pin,license · origin: mmx6 1.6/T8 · added: 2026-10-01

Sharing between a sibling game's C and this binary is measured, and any body adopted, under this binary's compiler pin
(`gcc2.95.2-psx-aspsx2.86`), not the sibling's own compiler. The control varies only the compiler. Only counts reach
tracked files; no sibling byte, object, signature or per-function list is committed (G12), and adopted bodies follow
G102 (proven-shared only, THIRD_PARTY.md credit).
provenance: mmx6 1.6 T8 (X4SHARE exact 2042 of 8534 under the pin vs 0 under cc1 2.6.3; exact >= 8 words 1547 vs 47 under mmx4's 2.7.2)
