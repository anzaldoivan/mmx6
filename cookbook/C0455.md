# C0455 — A refused member's declaration cause is often its whole-unit feed: unitcut before declaration fixes
tags: refusecause,unitcut,relocdef,declaration,path,repair,dedup · date: 2026-11-13 · phase/task: 3.1/T19 · origin: mmx6 3.1 T19.c1 + expert control

Symptom: refusecause gives a member `declaration` (R2 conflicting types or too few arguments) or `path`. The conflict sits in other functions of the exemplar's unit, not in the shared body.

Cause: the registry's shared path is the exemplar's whole split unit. The member's block compiles every function of it against the member unit's declarations.

Move: rerun with `--repair unitcut` first. It feeds the exemplar's range only (C0439). Then add `--repair relocdef` for the callee and data renames it exposes. Do not start with declaration unification.

Evidence (3.1 T19): rock_35 at HEAD has 33 rows. Switches off: 32 declaration, 1 reloc. Unitcut alone: 31 reloc, 2 declaration. Unitcut with relocdef: 28 gated with R5 green.

Over all programs, 341 of T10's 596 declaration rows and 150 of its 229 path rows gate (campaign/refusecause.after.tsv). The holdout banks confirmed 11 of 14 dry-run gates through the real bank path.

Caveat: a gated member of a pinned exemplar still cannot bank pin-free.
