# Bank under a caller-fixed `M2C_UNK` prototype: a u8 parameter's `andi` comes back from a `u8 x = argN;` local

- Symptom: the gate stops on `conflicting types`. Callers in the same TU (and in other units) already declare `M2C_UNK f(M2C_UNK, M2C_UNK, M2C_UNK)`, so the body must take that signature. A declsync of every caller is program-wide (C0361).
- Taking the signature and casting at the use (`(u8)arg1` at the store) loses the prologue `andi 0xff`: 163/176.
- Move: declare a local `u8 sel = arg1;` initialised at its declaration, and use `sel`. The `andi` is back in the prologue: 176/176.
- On the same function: a shared row pointer (`p = table[arg0]`) fixed a length residual (+20). Then a C0135 store-order sweep, 72 single-line moves, fixed the sched residual (+19). The move put one global store first in its block.
