# C0124 — m2c guesses callee arity per body: call through an unprototyped cast; trust the TU's consensus declaration
tags: m2c,prototype,arity,cast,declaration,scaffold · date: 2026-10-04 · phase/task: 1.8/T5 · origin: mmx6 scaffold lane, W4

- m2c infers each callee's arguments from live registers per body, so bodies included into one TU disagree (`too few arguments`, conflicting types), and scaffold args are often stale registers (an extra third arg, a spurious `(s8)`).
- Recovery at bank time: call through an unprototyped cast (`((void (*)())f)(a, b)`); recovered 136 of 136 byte-exact in the scaffold lane (1.8/T5).
- When drafting by hand, take the TU's consensus declaration and the twin bodies' calls over m2c's guess (1.8/T13 c7, c17). Lever side: C0104.
