# Probe and plateau scores are not the gate verdict
- A probe-pack match does not guarantee a gate match. A body that matched every word in the probe pack can fail the gate on a length residual. Check the gate verdict, never the probe alone.
- A plateau residual can rate a wrong-length draft far above a probe-pack result. Check the instruction count against the target first.
- When the residual is pure scheduling order, a positional (order-aware) scorer beats the order-blind m/n plateau metric.
