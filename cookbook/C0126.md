# C0126 — A family differing in one non-relocated immediate gates from one match by a macro-parameterised body
tags: dedup,family,macro,parameterised,propagate,immediate · date: 2026-10-04 · phase/task: 1.8/T6 · origin: mmx6 W-fam

- Problem (C0071): When family siblings differ in an unrelocated field, relocation remap gates few of them.
- Technique: Lift the immediate to `#ifndef P / #define P <exemplar value> / #endif` (and `#undef P` at file end). The exemplar's dup class includes the body unchanged. Bank each sibling with `--define P=<k>`. Read k from the differing word's imm16 (signed).
- Result: Family 40505e80 went from 0 of 258 → gated by one hand match (1.8/T6). The tool is family_remap.py (docs/ops/decomp-environment.md).
