# C0098 — A spill-slot swap lever cannot be a pure order diff: declare a length 0 diff
tags: reproducer,spill,G-alloc,diff · date: 2026-10-02 · phase/task: 1.7/T5 · origin: mmx6 T5

Swapping two spill slots also moves their reload lines, so the a/b disassembly is not the same lines in another order. Declare `// diff: length 0` for slot-swap pairs.
