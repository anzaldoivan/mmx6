# A numerically pinned arg-register local for a global passed to an unprototyped call

Symptom: a u8/s8 global copied to another global and passed to an unprototyped call; a plain or u8 local double-loads or adds a `move`.
Lever: `register s32 v asm("$4") = GLOBAL;` then the store and the call with `v` (an s32 local pinned to the first argument register).
Cases: W22 rock_05 0x800F1788 (50/50) (T22.c37); same pin family closed rock_05 0x800E9CF8's hp constant ($2).
See also: C0141, C0161, C0179.
