# C0149 — Pin both values of two adjacent field copies to close a register swap
tags: lever,regalloc,pin,field,copy · date: 2026-10-06 · phase/task: 1.8.1/T9 · origin: mmx6 W9 rock_02 F2438

Symptom: two adjacent field copies (`dst->a = src->x; dst->b = src->y;`) come out with the registers of the two temporaries swapped. If you pin one value, the swap only moves to the other copy.

Lever: put block-scope numeric register pins on BOTH values, `{ register s32 t asm("$2") = src->x; register s32 u asm("$3") = src->y; dst->a = t; dst->b = u; }` (read the numbers off the target). This lever extends C0141 (pins on constants) to copied values. A one-pin variant on a single field read closed rock_02:0x800F0294 (T9.c57).

Case: W9 rock_02:0x800F2438 (T9.c45, 83/83 at probe 18).
Related: C0141, C0142 (one reused local), C0134 (pin spellings verbatim accepts).
