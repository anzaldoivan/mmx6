## Design notes
- Line format: `<utc> P<n> <question> AGREE|DISAGREE|NOT-RUN <a> <b> of <N> <unit>`; NOT-RUN prints `- - of - -` and a stdout-only `harness: P<n> NOT-RUN: <exc>` line.
- N sources: P1/P7 corpus rows; P2 corpus `c` rows; P3 BINS outputs; P4 corpus asm|include_asm; P5 bound2.account nfun; P6 loadmap N.
- P2 linked side: small PROGBITS section reader of build/<p>.elf at [vram, end), probe.trim; masked compare under elf_function masks (probe's compare loop is inline in probe.probe(), so the 3-line compare is in harness).
- P3 asm unit = first sorted asm/SLUS_013.95/*.s; rebuild proven by .o st_mtime_ns increase, else NOT-RUN.
- Self-test plants: P1 extra name in A; P2 bit 31 of first compiled word; P3 one sha in B (no rebuild in the control: A = B = current hashes); P4 one program +1 in corpus counts; P5 a function start mid-function in inv (phantom); P6 extra yaml stem; P7 one member dropped from the census class. NOT-RUN control: a gather that raises.
