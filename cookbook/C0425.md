# Diffing a hash-red linked binary: confirm placement in the map first
- When a propagated or banked unit makes the program hash red, the unit often shifts. The member then links away from its retail vram.
- Reading the built binary at the retail vram compares a neighbour. Symptoms are a first word that differs with both sides absolute (often a neighbour's `jr ra`), or relocations resolving to odd symbols such as `__romPos`.
- Look the member up in `build/<prog>.map` first. If it is placed elsewhere, compare its masked object words (probe.elf_function) with retail. Equal words mean a placement (address) cause, and different words mean a body cause.
