# C0464 — A store-order pin on a constant: write the later field first, the constant through a block-scoped s32 local
tags: lever,pin,constant,store-order,cse,qimode,block-scope · date: 2026-11-14 · phase/task: 3.2/T3 · origin: mmx6 T3.c4 start_dash

## Problem
A `$3` pin holds a constant in the target's store order (3.2 T3.c4, start_dash 0x8003A9C4 `one` $3). Unpinned, the constant's QImode store is CSEd with an earlier constant and lands in the wrong register.

## Move
- Write the field the target stores later first in source: `self->air_action = 2;`.
- Then store the constant through a block-scoped `s32` local: `{ s32 one = 1; self->afterimage = one; }`.
- The SImode local escapes the QImode CSE, and the allocator picks the target register. Verify by the SLUS hash with a negative control (C0367).
