# A goto into the then-arm folds into one `||` condition, assignments inside the test

Problem: m2c writes `if (a) goto body; f = B(x); if (f & 4) { body: ... }` with a goto into the then-arm.

Move: one condition, the assignment kept inside it: `if (a || (f = B(x)) & 4) { ... }`. gcc 2.95 emits the same
short-circuit branches. Two tests that each `goto set1` also merge into one `||`.

Related: `if (t & m) goto add; v = t & 0x100;` becomes `v = t & m; if (v == 0) v = t & 0x100;`. A chain of gotos
to one shared store becomes nested if/else that assigns the value. A goto to the very next statement is deleted.

Limits: the fold can change block order. Then keep the goto, marked (shared EC974, EA178 `turn`, EC8BC).

Evidence: 2.5/T11.c2, shared role files. The `||` fold: F25F0, F2738, F37DC, F96E0, F0CF4 (1 of 6). The `v = t & m`
form: F10C0, F0EA4 `move`. Nested if/else: EFC4C `set_call`, F3354. Deleted: F3BF8. Each matched with sha OK.
