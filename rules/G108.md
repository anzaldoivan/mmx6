# G108 — A codegen claim cites the pinned compiler source and carries a byte-proven reproducer pair
id: G108 · group: G · status: active · tags: decomp,compiler-walls,citations,pin · origin: mmx6 1.7/T1-T6 · added: 2026-10-02

A claim about how cc1 makes code (a map row, a lever, a cookbook idiom) is entered only with two proofs on the pin
(`gcc2.95.2-psx-aspsx2.86`): its mechanism cites the staged source `/opt/gcc-2.95.2-src` in the audited format
(`src:gcc-2.95.2/<path>:<line> "<fragment>"`, checked by `gccsrc.py --check`), and its lever is an a/b reproducer pair
whose objects differ as declared (`repro.py --lever L<nn>` green). A citation from memory, from another gcc version,
or from an inherited cookbook is a lead until both hold; an inherited idiom whose pair compiles identical is dropped.
provenance: mmx6 1.7 T1-T6 (DK-52: a "2.7.2" source that was 2.8.1, 40 of 135 citations drifted; 3 of 7 BFM idioms
byte-identical under 2.95.2; BFM X4 store-flush moved from cse to alias.c). Builds on G55, G67.
