# C0096 — Pipe-delimited rows break on citation fragments containing a pipe
tags: docs,format,citations · date: 2026-10-02 · phase/task: 1.7/T4 · origin: mmx6 T4

A `|`-delimited row format (map rows, INDEX lines) splits wrongly when a quoted source fragment contains `|`. Choose a fragment of the cited line without the character.
