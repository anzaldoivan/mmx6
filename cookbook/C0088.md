# C0088 — lhu vs lh on a masked short: & in the expression is shortened. An int temp keeps lh
tags: idiom,sym-narrow-load,G-expr,rtl,shorten,short · date: 2026-10-02 · phase/task: 1.7/T6 · origin: BFM §329 re-proven

1. **Residual (tell)**: a `short` that is read and masked loads with `lhu` where the target has `lh` (or the reverse). Both sides keep the mask `andi`. `sym-narrow-load`
2. **Mechanism**: rtl (G-expr, the C front end). For `&` (bitwise ops: shorten = -1), build_binary_op looks through the int promotion of `*p`. It does the AND on the HImode value. The load is then a plain movhi (`lhu`). An `int` temp hides the conversion, so the AND is on a sign-extended SImode value (`lh`).

3. **Lever**: `return (*p) & 0xFFF;` gives `lhu`. `int t = *p; return t & 0xFFF;` gives `lh`. The length is the same.
