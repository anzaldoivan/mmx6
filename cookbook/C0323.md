# C0323 — Raw views of a homed struct global to member access: what is safe, what moves bytes
tags: struct,homed,view,member,sign,divide,pointer,lbu · date: 2026-11-01 · phase/task: 2.5/T7 · origin: 2.5 T7.c1

Use: converting the per-file raw views of a homed struct global to member access without moving a byte.
Typical views: `*(T*)&g`, `T* v = (T*)&g` read as `v[k]`, and `extern s8 g_plus4`.

1. Safe in 2.5/T7 (96 files, bytes identical): the sign of a byte member (s8 or u8) in compares and stores.
   Also safe: an s16 view of an s8 member read as a truth value. Also safe: byte-pointer locals read only as `v[k]`.
2. These moved bytes. A word member at the other sign under `/` or `%` changes the divide sequence.
   Keep `*(u32*)&g` there and record the view. Pointer arithmetic on a byte-pointer local (`q = p + 7`) changed the
   schedule when written as `&g.member`. A low-byte read `*(u8*)&g & n` is an lbu, but `g.word & n` is an lw.
   Register-pinned locals (`register T* p asm("$16")`) were not tried.
3. Convert in batches, with one `make fleet-quick` for each batch. Bisect a red batch by reverting file by file.
4. Record every kept view in the struct's doc, under `## Views kept`. Name the fleet log that showed red.
