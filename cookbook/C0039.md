# C0039 — asm-differ arch mips is big-endian; PSX needs mipsel (m2c target mipsel-gcc-c)
tags: asm-differ,m2c,mips,endianness,psx · date: 2026-10-01 · phase/task: 1.4/T4 · origin: mmx6 T4

asm-differ's `arch: mips` decodes big-endian words; a PSX binary needs `mipsel`. Likewise m2c's target is `mipsel-gcc-c`. The wrong arch gives plausible-looking garbage, not an error.
