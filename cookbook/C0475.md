# C0475 — Pins on hoisted loop constants: set-once REG_EQUIV constants get doubled live length in greg
tags: regalloc,greg,local-alloc,priority,reg-equiv,loop,hoist,constant,pin,depin,wall · date: 2026-11-15 · phase/task: 3.2/T6 · origin: mmx6 T6.c5 func_80025984

Mechanism (gcc 2.95.2 local-alloc.c:870-874, confirmed in dumps): allocation priority is floor_log2(refs)*refs/live.
A pseudo set once with a REG_EQUIV note has its live length doubled.
So constants hoisted out of a loop (C0416) rank low and lose their target registers to x, y and flags.
Old drafts pinned those constants to compensate.

Lever: stop the hoist or the note, not the statement order.
One reused `s32 n` that carries several loop constants (C0142) is set many times, so loop.c does not hoist them.
On func_80025984 that took the pin-free score from 35 to 213 of 294 and replaced 6 of 9 pins.
Literal constants instead of locals, and reordered statements, did not change the allocation.

Resolved in the T6 retry (294/294 pin-free). Give the constant more refs.
The flow pass adds the loop depth to each ref, so a `do {} while (0)` around the constant's uses lifts its priority.
Per-arm tails that jump2 merges back (C0451) also add refs.
Never wrap a tail that jump2 merges: loop notes are scheduling barriers (95 and 143 of 294).

For a pointer that loses its register, let its local first carry a value loaded into that register.
Here `spr = (u8*)*(s32*)(base + arg1*4);` then `base = base + (s32)spr;` put spr in its target register.
