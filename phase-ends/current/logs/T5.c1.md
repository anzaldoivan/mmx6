## Decisions / why
- Planted control = SLUS_013.95 c-empty functions func_800134C8 (match), func_800134D0 (mutation), func_800166FC (syntax error), TU 120A0. Banked C has no nonmatchings .s (checked: corpus `c` rows have no .s under asm/), so a real include_asm function would break once this lane banks it. c-empty rows are banked already and keep retail words via the corpus extent; the planted .s is generic (`jr $ra` + delay slot) under build/scaffold/selftest/asm/, not touching src/. Self-test also checks the corpus-extent retail words equal probe.retail_words for the match function.
- `--valid-syntax`: 60-row sample without it: match 4 compile 0 nocompile 56 (cause: `->unkN` on `void *`); with it: match 12 compile 29 nocompile 19.
- Prelude as `#define` only-when-used: first gate run on 70 sample packs (typedef prelude) = 70 probe match, 70 `STOPPED R3: typecheck.py` (typedef outside include/mmx6/). After the change: banked 70 of 70.
- Retail extent from the corpus row, not probe.retail_words: find_extent walks all of asm/<prog> per call. Own make call, not probe.compile_obj: compile_obj copies to `.run/probe/<triple>/<basename>`, which collides across workers (same func name in two programs).
