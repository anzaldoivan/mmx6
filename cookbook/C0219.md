# Draw ledger and wave draw must share one pick function
- A ledger derived from one draw ordering and a wave drawn with another disagree on the component head.
- The member-of representative of the ledger and the leverage-ranked pick of the draw can differ. Then a component shows its drawn member as member-of and its refused member as undrawn.
- Derive both from one shared pick function with the same pre-verdict filters (journal, routing). Compare by component, not by row, before you count drawable work.
- Exemplar banking can turn unpropagated members from member-of back to undrawn: undrawn counts are not monotone under banking.
- Dropping a drifted refused row makes its member a plain undrawn target, unless its new key joins a class. Plan undrawn counts around registry drops.
