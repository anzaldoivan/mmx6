## For the expert
- Deviation: on failure GNU make itself appends `make: *** [Makefile:112: fleet] Error 1` after the FLEET line (the script's own last line is FLEET; on success FLEET is the last line). Unavoidable for any failing recipe short of exiting 0; gate readers should grep `^FLEET ` rather than take the literal last line on red runs.
