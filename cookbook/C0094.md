# C0094 — Line-numbered citations into C sources: split on \n only, never str.splitlines()
tags: python,citations,gcc,self-test · date: 2026-10-02 · phase/task: 1.7/T3 · origin: mmx6 T3

Python `str.splitlines()` also breaks at \f, \x1c-\x1e, \x85 and U+2028/2029. gcc sources carry form feeds, so every line number after one drifts. Split on "\n" and decode as latin-1; keep a form-feed control in the auditor's self-test.
