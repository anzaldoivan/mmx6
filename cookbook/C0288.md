# C0288 — Open draft stuck at R2/R4 in a shared unit: carve it into its own unit, then bank
tags: layout,carve,bank,r2,r4,rodata,unit · date: 2026-10-30 · phase/task: 2.4/T3 · origin: 2.4 T3.c1

# An open draft stuck at R2/R4 in a shared unit often banks alone in its own unit

Problem: a draft in a large shared .c unit stops at R2 or R4.
- R2: `previous definition` or `conflicting types`. A sibling earlier in the unit declares the target differently.
- R4: `CARVE REFUSE foreign-rodata`. The unit's .rodata holds other functions' bytes.

Move: carve the target into its own unit, then bank with the normal gate.
1. `carve.py opt <prog> <tu> <vram>`, then `carve.py opt <prog> <newtu> <next fn>`.
2. Move the `rodata` rows whose dispatcher is past the split. Carve opt moves only `jtbl` rows.
3. Re-split (C0289), rebuild, run corpus, then bank.

Evidence (2.4 T3, docs/ops/layout-test.md):
- Open 1.x drafts: 8 of 45 banked in the shared unit, 25 of 45 in their own unit.
- R2 stops fall from 24 to 0. The rodata recovery passes 27 times.
- Sibling prototype mismatches then show as R3 in declsync (10 targets).
- 3 of 75 carves turned the hash red. The gate decides: revert such a carve.
