# C0381 — Splitting a fused asm symbol at a callback start trips th-bound2 phantom and th-ledger missing row
tags: split,boundaries,bound2,ledger,symbols,fleet,asm · date: 2026-11-06 · phase/task: 2.6/T23 · origin: mmx6 T23.c2

Trap (mmx6 2.6/T23): splitting a fused asm symbol at a by-address callback start (sized `type:func` symbols, C0048)
is byte-neutral, but the fleet goes red twice:
- th-bound2: `phantoms=1` ghidra-missed-start at the new start. Fix: a `config/boundary_exceptions.txt` row (T15.c1 form).
- th-ledger: `LEDGER BAD <prog>:0x<vram> missing`. Fix: a `campaign/ledger.tsv` row.
- And the split alone is `asm` +1 (a down kind): commit it only with a bank of at least one half.
A split commit therefore carries: symbols, C refs `D_<vram>` -> `func_<vram>`, the new INCLUDE_ASM,
the boundary exception and the ledger row.
