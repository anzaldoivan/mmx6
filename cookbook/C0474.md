# C0474 — A retail register copy of a just-loaded value: an inlined matched callee reloading the same word
tags: lever,copy,cse,inline,callee,scratchpad,pin,depin,regalloc · date: 2026-11-15 · phase/task: 3.2/T6 · origin: mmx6 T6.c12 func_80025E1C

Symptom: retail loads a word (here the draw-buffer index from the scratchpad) and keeps a register copy of it.
One register feeds some uses and the copy feeds others.
Our draft has no copy, so it is one word short and every later word is shifted.
Old drafts faked the copy with register pins.

Mechanism: two loads of the same word, one in the function and one in an inlined callee, are merged by cse.
The merge leaves one load plus a register copy, and that copy is what retail has.

Lever: find a matched sibling callee in the codebase that reads the same word (grep src for the address or name).
Write that part as the callee's body, inlined, with its own load of the word.
Keep the outer function's own load for its other uses.

Evidence: 3.2 T6.c12 func_80025E1C went from 3/70 unpinned to 70/70 pin-free at probe 3.
The callee was func_80026128 (src/SLUS_013.95/80026028.c), an OT reset.
