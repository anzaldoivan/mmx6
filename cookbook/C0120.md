# C0120 — Predecrement-and-test on an s16 field without a temp keeps retail's lone zero test
tags: lever,predecrement,s16,zero-test,field,temp · date: 2026-10-04 · phase/task: 1.8/T13 · origin: mmx6 W4

1. **Residual (tell)**: Retail tests the decremented field once against zero and stores late. A temp-based draft reorders the decrement or adds a compare.
2. **Mechanism**: This is observed. `if (--obj->s16field == 0)` with later reads of the field (no temp) keeps one zero test and the late decrement store.
3. **Lever**: Write the predecrement inside the condition. Read the field again afterwards instead of caching it.
4. **Byte proof**: The proof is in wave W4 (1.8/T13 c11), banked.
