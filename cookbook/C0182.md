# Pin a compare result through a comma form when a pinned value dies at its last compare

Symptom: the `slti` result reuses the register of a numerically pinned value that dies at that compare; retail puts it
elsewhere.
Lever: pin the compare result itself: `(c = n < 4, c)` with `c` a block local pinned to the target register, or
`(e = y < d, e) == 0` in the head. Related: a clamp `if (f < K) f = K;` with K needing lui wants both the field read and
K pinned in one block (pinning K alone moves the read to a third register) (C0141 family).
Limit seen: a shared `%hi` across a head compare and a tail reload, where retail copies the register in a delay slot,
did not respond to any spelling, pin, ladder or goto form in ~60 variants: treat as plateau after a short sweep.
