# Negative constant to a u16 field: store through an s16 view for the sign-extended constant load

Symptom: a store of a negative constant (e.g. -0x20) to a u16 field loads the constant zero-extended. Retail loads it sign-extended.
Lever: store through an s16 view of the field (`*(s16 *)&p->f = -0x20;` or an s16 view macro) instead of the u16 member.
Cases: W23 rock_22 0x800EB104 (93/94 → 94/94) (T23 W23, port of banked twin func_800EAF94).
See also: C0119 (s16 to s32 before negating into a halfword), C0137 (signed-byte spellings).
