# C0380 — Loop-invariant masks hoist in first-use order: operand order of (a&M1)|(b&M2) picks the lui order
tags: lever,loop,invariant,hoist,mask,sched,order · date: 2026-11-06 · phase/task: 2.6/T23 · origin: mmx6 T23.c2

Lever (mmx6 2.6/T23, func_80013530 half, 139 -> 147/150).
- gcc 2.95.2 (-O2) hoists loop-invariant masks (0xFFFFFF, 0xFF000000) into the preheader.
  The hoist order is the order of first use in the loop body, not declaration order.
- So the operand order of `(a & M1) | (b & M2)` decides which mask's `lui/ori` comes first and which reg it takes.
- Swapping to `t = (mode->x & 0xFF000000) | ((u32)tile & 0xFFFFFF)` fixed the `mode->x` load (+8 words).
  The residual 3 is the hoist order itself, reversed by the same swap.
- Also inert here: function-scope mask locals (C0106 shape), reg pins (C0141), order sweeps (C0135).
- Permuter at 3 residual: 12 seeds x ~100 it, all stuck (C0373).
- T29 dumps (dumps.py --src): `.cse` emits each mask's `(set (reg) const)` at its first expanded use.
  There insn 123 (0xFF000000) precedes insn 127 (0xFFFFFF).
  `.loop` moves them to the preheader in body order (456, 458), so the order is fixed at expand.
  Closed by the P_TAG bitfield form (C0399), not by operand order.
