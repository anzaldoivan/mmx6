# C0303 — Split runs: static-only entries join the next run, and a closing #undef stays with the previous role
tags: layout,split,static,undef,role,runs · date: 2026-10-31 · phase/task: 2.4/T15.1 · origin: mmx6 T15.1.c1

Context: layout.py runs_of and build (2.4 T15.1.c1).
- A static helper entry has no named non-static function. It belongs to its caller, which follows it in retail order.
- Attaching it to the previous run left the banner and helper of `memcard_list_files` in save_file.c (DC1 refusal).
- Rule: static-only entries join the run after them. At unit end they join the run before.
- At a role boundary the closing `#undef` lines of a body stay with the previous role. Blank lines may precede them.
- The next role file then opens on its own banner.
- Verify: `layout.py --self-test`, then apply and the token check (C0291).
