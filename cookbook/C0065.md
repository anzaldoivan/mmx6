# C0065 — Parameterise a shared body per member with name #defines; gate with an unmasked linked compare
tags: dedup,propagate,define,relocation · date: 2026-10-01 · phase/task: 1.6/T5 · origin: mmx6 T5

When dup-class members share code but differ in relocated symbols, instantiate one shared body per member with `#define <exemplar fn> <member>` and `#define <exemplar table> D_<absolute addr>` (addr from the member's retail hi/lo pair), include, then `#undef`. The masked probe cannot catch a wrong target; a per-member unmasked compare of the linked extent does.
