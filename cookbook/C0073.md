# C0073 — Test sibling-game sharing under the target's compiler pin. Vary only the compiler as the control
tags: prior-art,sibling-game,pin,control · date: 2026-10-01 · phase/task: 1.6/T8 · origin: mmx6 T8

To measure whether a sibling game's C shares bodies with the target binary, compile that C under the target's pin. Do not compile it under the sibling's own compiler. As the control, change only the compiler. Exact counts should track the target pin. Here, the counts are 1547 exact >= 8 words under X6's 2.95.2 vs 47 under mmx4's 2.7.2.
