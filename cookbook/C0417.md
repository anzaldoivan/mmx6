tags: lever,combine,andi,lbu,mask,nonzero_bits,local · date: 2026-11-08 · phase/task: 2.7/T11 · origin: mmx6 T11.c5 func_800232D4

# A redundant `andi 0xff` after `lbu` in retail means a local set twice
- Symptom: retail masks a byte it has just loaded with `lbu` (`andi 0xff`), and our draft does not.
- Mechanism: combine drops the mask when it knows the register's nonzero bits. A pseudo set twice has no nonzero_bits record, so the mask stays.
- Move: share one wider local across both sets (`u32 idx` assigned twice) and cast at the test (`if ((u8)idx == 0xFF)`). A use that needs no mask reads the raw value.
- mmx6 2.7 T11.c5 func_800232D4: the last step from 419 aligned to MATCH 446/446. The early return also reads `obj->unk16` directly. See C0078 for the reverse case (lw+andi vs lbu).
