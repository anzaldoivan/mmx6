# Scope a layout propose to a task's files: merge rows, keep the rest of HEAD

After a tree-wide rename batch, `layout.py propose` changes rows far outside the task (2.6 T14.c9: 202+/172-,
22 new stems refused first as `LAYOUT unknown-system`).
- First add each refused stem to a docs/layout.md "## SLUS systems" row (glossary stem or noun fallback).
- Merge: keep every row whose (prog, file, vram, system) is unchanged, plus new rows over the task's files only.
  A pure unit relabel stays (HEAD may predate a c_units split).
- Revert a split whose remainder gets a new address name: filename debt does not fall.
- A forced collision rename (`<stem>_<vram>.c`) of a finished file is fine: its filename debt stays 0.
- After apply: mirror deletions on the host (C0299). Restore line-1 banners that apply pushed down (DC1).
  Then shift cites by the banner line in verdicts and basis, and re-run namesqueue check.
