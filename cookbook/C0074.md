# C0074 — Draw filter over a twin graph: at most one member per connected component per wave
tags: draw,twins,wave,routing · date: 2026-10-01 · phase/task: 1.6/T9 · origin: mmx6 T9

A draw filter over a twin graph (exact + near) must cap a wave at one member per connected component, else two members of one open cluster are drafted together and one is wasted. The component count, not the list length, bounds a wave.
