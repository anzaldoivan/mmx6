# Calibrate propagate's grouped dry-run cost on a full-size group, not a small key sample

- Trap (2.7 T4): 20 SLUS keys grouped took 267 s (13.4 s/unit). A 451-unit SLUS group was still running after 6.5 h (>= 52 s/unit). Per-unit cost grows with group size, so a small sample underestimates by 3x or more.
- The SLUS group runs serially in one worker (largest first), so it sets each shard's wall. Rock groups spread over 8 workers.
- Reading the walls: `PROPAGATE GROUP <prog> units u isolated i fallback yes|no wall Ns` is printed per program as each one finishes. A least-squares fit over the rock groups gave 16.4 s per grouped unit and 22.6 s per isolated unit. The worst group ran at 40 s/unit.
- Refused-heavy keys are cheap: 284 refused rows in one program form one isolated unit (88 s worst).
- Model in tools/mmx6/thshard.py. Numbers in docs/ops/selftest-cost.md "Shards of the full chain".
