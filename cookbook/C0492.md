# C0492 — unitcut reuse for a key's next member: a split unit's cut body names its including unit
tags: propagate,unitcut,uc_due,split-unit,reuse,rolefeed,R2 · date: 2026-11-18 · phase/task: 3.3/T31 · origin: mmx6 3.3 T31.c1

When: under `--repair unitcut`, a key's first member cut the exemplar into its own body. A later member then refuses R1 or R2 without `unitcut reused` in its note. It can be in the same program or a later one in the same live tree. It was fed the whole unit.

- Cause: bank.uc_due reused an earlier cut only when the body's marker named src itself. The marker is `// rolefeed (3.1 T4): <f> of <unit>`. For a split unit (8004FF7C.c, 800E98B0.c) it names the including unit (4FF7C.c, E9860.c). So uc_due went false, and the member was fed all of src.
- Fix (3.3 T31.c1): uc_due also reuses the cut when src #includes the body (the line rolefeed wrote).
- Proof: live runs from the pre-paste tree (630b6a69^) show it.
  - 3841861b: 2 of 28 before, 6 of 28 after (rock_35 0x800FCAD0 and 0x800FE2D0, rock_44 0x800FDA98 and 0x80100C60).
  - b15f90b3 rock_20 0x800F1D28 and a0325082 rock_20 0x800F2EB4 gate too.
  - rock_18 0x800EF964 moves from R1 0/37 to its real wall: R2 `conflicting types for D_800F0F80`.
- A dry run or refusecause never shows this: each runs a member in a fresh tree, so every member cuts afresh.
