# Naming batch: holds with no queue key, and queue drift

- `namesqueue.py build` refuses a lead whose candidate is the placeholder itself (`placeholder-tier`) or a non-name such as `g_Player.hp` (`style`). Such holds have no queue key, so they cannot be verdict rows.
- Record them as comment lines in `config/names-verdicts.tsv` under a section header, `# held <prog> <vram> <token>: <clause>` (precedent: the proto L1/L2 comments). `check` skips comments, so the count stays n of n.
- Run `namesqueue.py build` before `check`. `check` reads the committed queue, and a rebuild can queue rows from other tasks' edits (2.6 T7: 6 tweaks rows from hoisted globals). Hold those with an explicit "not judged" clause, or judge them.
- Held vrams can carry older verdict rows from another source (rejected tweaks or archipelago leads). Match on (vram, this batch), not on vram alone, when you check coverage.
