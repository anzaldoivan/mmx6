# Zero test of a decremented s16 global: spell it as a mask for the zero-extend form

When: retail decrements an s16 global and tests the result for zero with a zero-extending halfword view.
The compare reads the low 16 bits unsigned. With `== 0` or a `(u16)` cast, gcc keeps its sign-extend form.

Lever: `g--; if (!(g & 0xFFFF)) …` — the mask is shortened to the unsigned halfword view (cf. C0088 for the
inverse case: an int temp keeps the signed load).

Evidence: W24 rock_04 func_800F4C68 (T24): `== 0` and `(u16)g == 0` both gave 45/46. The mask spelling gave 46/46.
Try alongside C0120 (s16 pre-decrement in the test) when a counter's zero test differs only in extension.
