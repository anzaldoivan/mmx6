# C0081 — Load held below *p= but hoisted above p->f= (sched /s aliasing)
tags: idiom,sym-load-below-store,G-sched,sched,aliasing,order · date: 2026-10-02 · phase/task: 1.7/T5 · origin: mmx6 repro

1. **Residual (tell)**: a global `lw` stays after a store through a pointer. At that place, the target hoists it above the store (or the reverse). The instructions are the same, but their order differs. `sym-load-below-store`
2. **Mechanism**: sched (G-sched). A load depends on a pending store only if true_dependence says they may alias. A member store (/s, in-struct) cannot alias a fixed scalar. A bare `*p` store can.
   - src:gcc-2.95.2/gcc/sched.c:1393 "if (true_dependence (XEXP (pending_mem, 0), VOIDmode,"
   - src:gcc-2.95.2/gcc/alias.c:1269 "return !fixed_scalar_and_varying_struct_p (mem, x, varies);"
   - src:gcc-2.95.2/gcc/expr.c:4770 "MEM_SET_IN_STRUCT_P (to_rtx, 1);"
   - a-side `.sched`: the store insn is first, `(insn 12 19 14 (set (mem:SI (reg:SI 4 a0) 0)`. The b-side has `(set (mem/s:SI (reg:SI 4 a0) 0)` after the load.
3. **Lever**: the form of the store. `*p = v` holds the load below. `p->a = v` (a struct pointer, and a member at offset 0 gives the same `sw`) lets sched hoist it. This is the same family as C0075 (cse reload). Inertness checked: b has the same lines in another order.
4. **Byte proof**: repro/G-sched/ldst.{a,b}.c. `repro.py --lever L07` gives `LEVER L07 OK`. The compile is standalone under the pin. Retail anchor: none yet. Map row L07.
