# Constant dividend loaded before an abs branch: write the abs as a ternary inside the divide expression

Symptom: a divide of a constant by an absolute value; the target materialises the constant dividend before the abs branch, ours after it (sched residual).
Cause: an abs written as separate if-statements on a local lets the constant propagate to the divide site.
Lever: `q = K / ABS(x);` with ABS a ternary macro inside the expression keeps the constant in the block before the branch. Pinned dividend locals did not help.
Evidence: W13 rock_08 F2814 (c33 87/87, 5 probes).
Related: C0152 (divisor in a local), C0144.
