# C0470 — Shard cost per key range: calibrate the SLUS rate from each timed shard's wall/units
tags: propagate,shard,thshard,calibration,timing,tools-health-full · date: 2026-11-15 · phase/task: 3.2/T11 · origin: mmx6 3.2 T11 thshard refit

Context: the thshard.py shard cost model (C0421, C0422). A flat SLUS rate of 20 s/unit gave every full shard the same 2320 s estimate. T1's 11 shards ran 1782-3434 s. Key count does not predict cost.

Move (3.2 T11):
- Calibrate per key range. Each timed shard becomes a row in thshard.py SLUS_CAL: first key prefix, grouped wall, SLUS units.
- A key costs wall/units of the range it sorts into (bisect on the sorted keys). New keys take their neighbours' rate.
- So the expensive range splits on its own. Shard 2 had 7 R5 rounds and 40 cross-key bisects, at ~34 s/unit.
- Use the slower of repeated runs for a range (shard 2: 3434 s, rerun 3156 s).
- Pick SPLIT_AT by a scan of the greedy split on the real key list (host: the PROPAGATE_KEYS_CMD awk piped to `thshard.py check`).
- Greedy re-flow fills shards up to the threshold. 2550 gave 11 shards near the budget. 2500 gave 12, max est 2499 s under 3000 s.
- Refit SLUS_CAL from the GROUP lines of the next timed ALL run (`PROPAGATE GROUP SLUS_013.95 ... wall Ns`).
