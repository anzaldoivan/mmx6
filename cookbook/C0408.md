# C0408 — OR operand order picks the expansion order: write the clut's % 16 term first
tags: lever,operand-order,or,clut,psyq,sprite,order · date: 2026-11-07 · phase/task: 2.7/T1 · origin: mmx6 T1.c3 func_800144DC

Lever (mmx6 2.7/T1.c3, func_800144DC, 97 -> 100/100, no pins). gcc 2.95.2 -O2.
- Shape: a PsyQ clut word built from one byte, `(c % 16) | ((0x1E0 + c / 16) << 6)`.
- The operand spelled first in the OR is expanded first. Retail computes the `andi 0xF` term before the
  `addiu`/`sll` term, so the `% 16` term goes first.
- The `/ 16` term first, or a `* 64 +` spelling, scored 97. Open-coded `& 0xF` / `>> 4` matched too.
- Same family as C0146 (add operand order) and C0380 (mask OR order): try both OR operand orders.
