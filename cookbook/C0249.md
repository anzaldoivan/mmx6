# A vote-derived alias cannot corroborate the votes that made it

Context: x4names `--promote` (1.10 T6) admits an mmx4 name only with one recorded X6 corroborator. The relocation-slot
votes of `--widen` (.run/x4names/votes.tsv) give two kinds. Caller: (another anchor's slot names this address). Callee:
(this function's own slot lands on an address carrying the voted name).

Trap: a tier `slot` row exists *because of* those votes (>= 2 voters, the row's own function among them).
Such a row is a circular callee witness. 1.10 T6.c1 admitted 40 rows, 8 of them only through slot rows such as
`vram_rect_ptr`.

Move: callee targets come from independently proven rows only (`x4 exact` basis). Caller witnesses are other
anchors, each with its own exact partnership. Fixed at 1a6e48cb: 32 admitted (caller 4, callee 26, data 2).

General: when a derived fact feeds an admission check, list its inputs. If the candidate is among them, the fact is no witness.
