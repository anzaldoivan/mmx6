# C0043 — A break 7 near a div is not an expanded-div test; classify by break 6 presence and mflo position
tags: mips,division,maspsx,expand-div,fingerprint · date: 2026-10-01 · phase/task: 1.4/T6 · origin: mmx6 T6

maspsx `--expand-div` adds a `break 6` overflow check to a signed div macro; gcc 2.95 emits its own zero trap (`bne b,$0; nop; break 7`) and schedules other instructions between `div` and the trap. A window test (`break 7` within N insns) misclassifies. Classify by `break 6` presence and `mflo` position.

Source: phase 1.4 T6 (optscan div-bare was a window artifact).
