# C0245 — Check a depin sweep with debt --files, because depin counts differ from the floor
tags: depin,pin-unmarked,debt,fake,clang-format · date: 2026-10-24 · phase/task: 1.10/T2 · origin: mmx6 T2 remark

- depin.py prints its own pin totals. Those totals do not prove that the floor sees every survivor as marked.
- After a sweep, run `psxd_floor.py debt --files` on the host. List the swept files that still show `pin-unmarked`.
- Two causes were found in 1.10/T2. Adjacent kept pins got one marker for two pins (a depin bug, fixed in T2).
- An old trailing `// !FAKE:` on a pin line that clang-format wrapped is not seen by depin. Move the marker to its own line above the pin.
- depin skips a wrapped pin line (`asm(` at line end), so its count can be lower than the floor count.
- Run the clean rebuild after the marker fix. A comment-only change must still hash OK.
