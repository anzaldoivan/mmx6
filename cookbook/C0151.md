# C0151 — Compare operand order follows the spelling: write x > tbl[i], not tbl[i] < x
tags: lever,operand-order,compare,sched · date: 2026-10-06 · phase/task: 1.8.1/T9 · origin: mmx6 W9 rock_02 F612C

Symptom: a relational compare between a field and a table entry (or two globals) loads its operands in the wrong order; the residual is a few rows of load scheduling around the compare.

Lever: swap the compare's operand order in the source, `if (f6 > tbl[f5])` instead of `if (tbl[f5] < f6)`; gcc 2.95 expands the operands left to right and the schedule keeps it. Same family as C0146 (add operand order follows the spelling).

Cases: W9 rock_02:0x800F612C (T9.c50, 72/79 to 79/79); rock_05:0x800EFAD4 (T9.c34, `y > D + off`); rock_05:0x800EF7D4 (T9.c28, global-vs-global compare).
Related: C0146, C0135.
