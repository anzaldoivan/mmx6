# C0100 — decomp-permuter --seed random-walks one candidate, and seed 0 means unseeded
tags: permuter,seed,random · date: 2026-10-02 · phase/task: 1.7/T7 · origin: mmx6 T7

With `--seed S`, the permuter random-walks a single candidate. Once the walk reaches a non-compiling state, it stays at score `inf`. `--seed 0` is upstream's "no seed". Refuse it in wrappers that promise reproducibility.
