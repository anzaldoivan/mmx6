# C0297 — splat writes text-section data .s only at a spimdisasm boundary in its segment
tags: splat,spimdisasm,carve,include_asm,dlabel · date: 2026-10-31 · phase/task: 2.4/T23 · origin: mmx6 T23.c1 rock_22

# splat writes a text-section data .s only where spimdisasm sees a function boundary in that segment

- In a whole c unit, text-section data labels (`dlabel`, e.g. rock_22 FC2B8/FC488/FD0CC) get their own nonmatchings .s.
- After a split, in the new segment spimdisasm folds them into the preceding function's .s as `alabel`. INCLUDE_ASM for them then fails: `can't open .../<seg>/func_<addr>.s`.
- References from another segment do not create the boundary. `symbols` rows (plain, `type:func`, `size:` on the preceding function) did not split them (2.4 T23, T5.c3).
- Consequence: do not place a split such that text-section data lands mid-segment without its own boundary. `carve.py opt --gate` reverts it. Open lever: a splat/spimdisasm boundary override.
Related: C0070, C0289.
