# G101 — Prior art is a lead until our own bytes prove it
id: G101 · group: G · status: active · tags: provenance, prior-art, evidence · origin: intake · added: 2026-10-01

## Text

Every fact taken from outside the project — a name or address from Kuumba123's lists, the overlay map from
MegaManX6Recomp, mmx4's compiler pin or struct names, a RAM variable from the Archipelago world, a table from acediez's
documentation — enters `docs/prior-art.md` as a row with its source, license and status: `lead` →
`consistent-with-bytes` (checked against our own dump or an oracle, the check cited) → `proven-at-gate` (a green byte
gate, or a runtime proof with three datapoints or a before/after diff, M3). A lead may order the work (which candidate
to probe first, where to look); it may not stand as a fact: nothing is banked on, recorded in the load map or the
toolchain pin, or used as a name from a row below `proven-at-gate`, and a name additionally needs its own recorded
basis (G62).

## Why

Leads mistaken for evidence were the intake's first named risk: a wrong pin, a misread overlay base or a borrowed name
produces systematic wrong work that no test catches, and the X4↔X6 sharing hypothesis is unproven.
