# Nested subtract with a constant: hold (b - K) in a numerically pinned local

Symptom: retail computes `a - (b - K)` as a subtract of b from a pre-adjusted value, or the reverse. Our build differs in operand order and register choice around the constant.

Cause: combine reassociates `a - (b - K)` into `(a + K) - b`. The compiler propagates a plain local that holds `b - K` back and folds it the same way.

Lever: put `b - K` in a block local with a numeric register pin. Then subtract the local. The pin keeps the subexpression as its own pseudo and picks the sum register.

Evidence: W15 rock_02 F33C0 (screen-left midpoint). A plain local did not move it. A pinned local matched.
Related: C0116 (local stops constant reassociation for an index), C0141 (numeric pins).
