# C0277 — Cross-unit masked twin: assign only a unique hit, count the ambiguous ones
tags: twins,masked,protodiff,proto,census · date: 2026-10-26 · phase/task: 2.3/T5 · origin: mmx6 T5.c1

Problem: a prototype function has no same-address retail match. Find its retail twin by masked body equality.
The mask covers relocations, jal targets and lui/addiu.

Move: index retail bodies (n>1 words) by masked body to a list of (unit, vram). Assign `moved` only when exactly one
hit exists. Count the ambiguous ones, never resolve them.

Evidence (2.3 T5, protodiff): 140 absent proto rows had a masked twin, and only 61 were unique.
A 15-word body masked equal to 674 retail functions. 1-word bodies (stub programs) are skipped outright.

Controls: a true pair with differing raw bytes (C0055), a near-twin with one register field flipped, a twin in
two units, a 1-word stub. All but the first must be refused.
