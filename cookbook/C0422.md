# C0422 — Shard wall variance comes from SLUS group R5 bisect rounds. Budget the estimate x1.3
tags: propagate,shard,thshard,R5,bisect,tools-health-full,timing · date: 2026-11-09 · phase/task: 3.0/T7 · origin: mmx6 T7.c1 shards 0..10

Context: tools-health-full by shard (`make tools-health-shard SHARD=k`), with the thshard.py cost model (SLUS 20 s/unit, cap 101 SLUS units per shard, C0421).

Observation (3.0 T7, 11 shards timed back to back after one fleet, logs .run/3.0/logs/t7c1-shard<k>.log):
- The SLUS_013.95 group is 97-99% of every propagate shard's wall. Rock groups (max ~900 s) run beside it.
- 8 of 10 shards needed 1 group R5 round and landed 9-27% under estimate.
- Shards 2 and 6 needed 7 and 9 rounds (red R5 bisect, C0418). They ran 28% and 19% over (2969 s and 2757 s, against 2320 s).

Technique:
- Read a shard's risk from its SLUS r5 round count (the `PROPAGATE GROUP SLUS_013.95 ...` lines), not from its key count.
- Budget the model estimate x ~1.3 for the bisect tail. Under the 3 h cap there is ample headroom today (max 2969 s).
- If a shard nears the cap, lower the SLUS unit cap first. Rounds scale with the SLUS group, not the rock groups.
