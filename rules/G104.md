# G104 — A pin is unique only over rungs a probe can distinguish; collapse byte-equivalent rungs first
id: G104 · group: G · status: active · tags: decomp,probe,pin,ladder · origin: mmx6 1.4/T6,T6.1 · added: 2026-10-01

A ladder of candidate triples claims a unique pin only over rungs a probe can tell apart. Before the ladder is run, rungs
whose codegen is identical on the target's idioms (e.g. maspsx aspsx 2.56-2.86 under -G0 with no $gp access) are
collapsed to one named representative with the mechanism recorded; a new rung for the class needs a probe that exercises
the distinguishing idiom. An "every other rung FAIL" check over byte-equivalent rungs is unsatisfiable by construction.
provenance: mmx6 1.4 T6 (PIN AMBIGUOUS over 4 aspsx rungs), T6.1 (class collapsed, PIN 3 of 3)
