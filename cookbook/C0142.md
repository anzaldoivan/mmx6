# C0142 — One reused s32 local carrying two values orders loads and stores and shares their register
tags: lever,local-reuse,sched,regalloc,global-alloc · date: 2026-10-05 · phase/task: 1.8.1/T6 · origin: mmx6 W6

Symptom: a tail of stores has a different schedule (a load hoisted above a store, two values in swapped registers). Separate temps plus order sweeps stall at a few words.

Lever: one function-scope s32 local carries two values in sequence. First example: an address, then a constant. Second example: a countdown loaded, decremented, stored, then reused for an increment. The reuse makes the second load wait for the first store and lets global alloc give both one register, as retail does.

Evidence: phase 1.8.1 T6 rock_06:0x800E9F04, rock_08:0x800EF340, rock_22:0x800F01DC (logs/T6.c<k>.md).
