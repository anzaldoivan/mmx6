# C0108 — Constant multiply spelled as shifted terms with a temp picks synth_mult's destination register
tags: lever,synth_mult,multiply,shift,regalloc,jump2 · date: 2026-10-03 · phase/task: 1.8/T10 · origin: mmx6 W1

1. **Residual (tell)**: A multiply by a constant such as 80 (`x*80`) is synthesised as two shifts plus an add. The sum lands in the other shift's register than retail.
2. **Mechanism**: gcc 2.95 `synth_mult` for `x*80` computes `(x<<6) + (x<<4)` and accumulates into the `x<<4` register. Source written as `x*64 + v`, with `v = x*16` computed first into its own temp, puts the sum in the `x<<6` register. It also lets jump2 cross-jump the arm's common store.
3. **Lever**: Spell constant multiplies as explicit shifted terms with a temp for the term computed first. To swap the destination register, swap which term gets the temp.
4. **Byte proof**: In wave W1 (1.8/T10), rock_02:0x800F38F4 went 377 → 379/379 (banked after a plumbing re-gate).
