# C0140 — An 8-byte-larger frame with no stack use: an unused s32 pad[2] local
tags: lever,frame,stack,pad,unused-local · date: 2026-10-04 · phase/task: 1.8.1/T5 · origin: mmx6 W5

Symptom: draft frame (`addiu sp, -N`) is 8 bytes smaller than retail; the body and the saved registers match.
Lever: declare an unused local array `s32 pad[2];` (size = the missing bytes); gcc 2.95 reserves its slots even unused at -O2.
Evidence: phase 1.8.1 T5 c10 rock_45:0x800FABC0, logs/T5.c10.md.
Check: the stack offsets of saved registers and spill slots must also move to retail's; if only the outgoing-args area differs, look at callee arg counts instead.
