# C0080 — $16/$17 swapped by ref count: global-alloc density floor_log2(refs)*refs/live
tags: idiom,sym-sreg-swapped,G-alloc,greg,regalloc,refs · date: 2026-10-02 · phase/task: 1.7/T5 · origin: mmx6 repro

1. **Residual (tell)**: two values that are live across calls sit in `$16`/`$17`, swapped against the target. Their ref counts differ. `sym-sreg-swapped`
2. **Mechanism**: greg (G-alloc). The global-alloc priority is `floor_log2(refs)*refs/live*10000`. In this pass, global-alloc allocates the value with the higher priority first, and that value gets `$16`.
   - src:gcc-2.95.2/gcc/global.c:603 "(floor_log2 (allocno_n_refs[v1]) * allocno_n_refs[v1])"
   - src:gcc-2.95.2/gcc/global.c:604 "/ allocno_live_length[v1])"
   - `alloc_table.py`: the a-side has x=83 refs 6 live 13 prio 9230 and y=87 refs 4 live 12 prio 6666 (x `$16`). The b-side has x=83 refs 4 prio 6153 and y=87 refs 6 prio 10000 (y `$16`). The a-side `.greg` has `;; 2 regs to allocate: 83 87`.
3. **Lever**: move references (an extra use, a re-read) to the value that must get `$16`, or shorten its live range. The floor_log2 steps (refs 3 -> 4, 7 -> 8) give the big jumps.
