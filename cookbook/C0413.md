# C0413 — PC switch shape scores low: enumerate case 0, group order, tail calls and division form
tags: lever,switch,jtbl,case-0,cross-jump,division,pc-port,generator · date: 2026-11-08 · phase/task: 2.7/T9 · origin: mmx6 T9.c9 func_8002F9DC

1. **Residual (tell)**: A PC port's /Od shape gives the right switch groups. Yet the draft scores far below the old one (19-31 against 79 of 132).
2. **Mechanism**: Small source details set the jtbl and the arm layout. gcc starts the table at the lowest case. A retail table from 0 then needs an explicit case 0. gcc cross-jumps two identical tail calls by itself. So one shared call does not match. A signed divide on an s16 rounds toward zero, and a shift does not.
3. **Lever**: Enumerate about 128 drafts with a small generator. Vary the flag form, the group order and the count of tail calls. Also vary the ternary arm order, divide or shift, and the param types. The two flag forms are `f = 0; if (t == 0x10) f = 1;` and `f = t == 0x10`.
4. **Byte proof**: 2.7 T9.c9, func_8002F9DC, on scratch rodata. 8 of 128 variants gave MATCH 132/132. Every match had the if-form flag, the 9-0xC group first, two tail calls, signed `/` and an `s16 a`. Not banked: the C0379 and C0393 carve walls (waves/W-t9c9/best.c).
5. **Related**: C0406, C0083 (cross-jump), C0379, C0393.
- Result 2.7 T9.c10 (func_800232D4, length plateau): probe words are positional, so they rank wrong-length drafts badly. A difflib alignment against retail (exact lines, then opcode only) ranked the generator's variants far better (waves/W-t9c10/asc.py, ignored).
