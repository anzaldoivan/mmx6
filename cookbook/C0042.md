# C0042 — A splat asm subsegment can come out as dlabel + .word with 0 glabel; per-function tools must detect it
tags: splat,spimdisasm,census,functions · date: 2026-10-01 · phase/task: 1.4/T5 · origin: mmx6 T5

spimdisasm sometimes treats an asm subsegment as data: the .s holds `dlabel` + `.word` lines and no glabel. A per-function tool then sees zero functions and reports an empty census silently. Detect 0-function programs and either carve functions (last `jr $ra` + delay slot, C0021) or flag them.

Source: phase 1.4 T5 (rock_17/43/45: 163 functions, 1044 words).
