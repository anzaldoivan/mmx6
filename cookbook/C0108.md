# C0108 — Constant multiply spelled as shifted terms with a temp picks synth_mult's destination register
tags: lever,synth_mult,multiply,shift,regalloc,jump2 · date: 2026-10-03 · phase/task: 1.8/T10 · origin: mmx6 W1

1. **Residual (tell)**: a multiply by a constant such as 80 (`x*80`) synthesised as two shifts plus an add, with the sum landing in the other shift's register than retail.
2. **Mechanism**: gcc 2.95 `synth_mult` for `x*80` computes `(x<<6) + (x<<4)` and accumulates into the `x<<4` register; source written as `x*64 + v` with `v = x*16` computed first into its own temp puts the sum in the `x<<6` register, and lets jump2 cross-jump the arm's common store.
3. **Lever**: spell constant multiplies as explicit shifted terms with a temp for the term computed first; swap which term gets the temp to swap the destination register.
4. **Byte proof**: wave W1 (1.8/T10), rock_02:0x800F38F4 377 → 379/379 (banked after a plumbing re-gate).
