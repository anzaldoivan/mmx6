# C0491 — unitcut in the exemplar's own program: R5 guards the cut body, not the unit
tags: propagate,unitcut,R5,sha1,same-unit,rolefeed,bank · date: 2026-11-18 · phase/task: 3.3/T31 · origin: mmx6 3.3 T31.c1

When: a live `propagate.py <key> --repair unitcut` refuses a same-program member `R5: body sha1 changed since R1 (<the exemplar's unit>)`, while refusecause (one member alone) gates it.

- Cause: the exemplar's record (gated[0]) hashes its whole unit at R1. Then a later member in the same program cuts the exemplar out of that unit. The cut body is src/shared/<prog>/func_<vram>.c. bank.rung5 (via propagate.rung5_unify) still compared the unit to the stale R1 hash.
- Fix (3.3 T31.c1): propagate.guarded(g, gated). Take a gated unitcut record whose saved holds g's src as it was at g's R1. Then R5 guards that record's cut body and R1 hash. A foreign edit still refuses: an edit of the unit before the cut, or of the cut body after it.
- Proof: live runs from the pre-paste tree (630b6a69^) show it.
  - b15f90b3: 2 of 4 before, 4 of 4 after (rock_18 0x800EEF88 gates).
  - a0325082: 2 of 4 before, 4 of 4 after (rock_18 0x800F0114 gates).
  - c68db282: rock_18 0x800EF8D0 gates too.
- Self-test: `control unitcut R5 + reuse (T31)` in propagate.py --self-test.
