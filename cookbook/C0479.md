# C0479 — An old wave plateau score is stale: port the draft to current names and prototypes first
tags: plateau,stale,ledger,rename,prototype,draft,pin,depin,wave · date: 2026-11-15 · phase/task: 3.2/T6 · origin: mmx6 T6.c2-c12 (W307)

Symptom: a plateau row's ledger score (from a wave months old) is far below what its draft really reaches.
Or the draft does not compile, or its register pins hide a different cause.

Cause: since the draft's wave, globals, fields and callees were renamed or retyped in include/ and src/shared.
A stale callee prototype with a wrong argument count makes a temp look like an argument register that needs a pin (C0471).

Step, before any lever: port the prior draft to the current headers and probe it once with its pins removed.
That unpinned, current-name score is the real base (C0375).

Evidence (3.2 T6, W307), row by row:
- func_8004DDD4: ledger 9/181, 170/181 after renames.
- func_80025E1C and func_8001717C: 3/70 and 25/104, both rose once names and prototypes were fixed.
- func_800192CC and func_8001717C: their pins only compensated for stale prototypes.
- func_80030ECC, func_80017630, func_80033214: the drafts needed renames to compile or score.
