# C0374 — permute.py reuses its run dir: in a seed loop, save best.c on the first score 0
tags: permuter,seed,best,overwrite · date: 2026-11-03 · phase/task: 2.6/T15 · origin: mmx6 T15.c3

## Problem
In a multi-seed loop (C0373), `permute.py` reuses `.run/permute/<func>/` for every run. A seed that reaches score 0 has its best.c overwritten by the next seed. The loop ends with no candidate on disk.

## Move
- Stop the loop, or copy `best.c` aside, on the first `PERMUTE CANDIDATE` / score 0.
- Otherwise rerun the winning seed alone: the walk is deterministic per seed (T15.c3: check_low_health seed 4, 100 it, reproduced best 0).

## Evidence
2.6 T15.c3 (`phase-ends/current/logs/T15.c3.md`): check_low_health best 0 at seed 4 of 12, gone after seed 12. A seed-4 rerun reproduced it. Banked e430b91b6.
