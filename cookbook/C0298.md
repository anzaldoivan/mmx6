# A split tail unit needs the file-scope declarations made inside earlier member bodies

- Banked member bodies of a c unit (.c files pulled in with #include) may declare file-scope `extern`s, prototypes, or `#include "x4.h"` that later bodies rely on.
- Splitting copies only the unit preamble. The tail then fails (`undeclared`, or a struct tag first seen in prototype scope → `conflicting types`). Seen 2.4 T23 rock_39 (`extern s16 D_80097202;` in rock_10/func_800F60AC.c, and x4.h).
- carve.py split_c now carries `member_decls`: extern/prototype/`#include "<x>.h"` lines from head member bodies, block #defines applied, lines using body-own macros skipped, deduped. Sound because the tail sees a subset of what it saw whole. The gate arbitrates the rest.
