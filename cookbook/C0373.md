# C0373 — A seeded permute.py walk dies to inf within ~70 iterations: split the cap into short multi-seed runs
tags: permuter,seed,random,inf,cap · date: 2026-11-03 · phase/task: 2.6/T15 · origin: mmx6 T15.c2

permute.py always passes a nonzero `--seed`, so upstream random-walks one candidate (C0100). On six SLUS movement functions (2.6 T15.c2) every walk reached a non-compiling state between iteration 8 and iteration 71. From then on it scored `inf` to the cap: 792 to 1829 of 850 to 1899 iterations were wasted. A 20-minute single seeded walk is therefore mostly dead time.

Lever: split the cap into many short seeded runs (seeds 1, 2, 3 and on, about 100 iterations each). Keep the best score across them. Each restart begins again from the base.
