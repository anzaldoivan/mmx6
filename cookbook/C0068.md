# C0068 — A planted .word .s must span the corpus extent to its end, or the link shifts
tags: plant,probe,extent,link · date: 2026-10-01 · phase/task: 1.6/T6.1 · origin: mmx6 T6.1

Retail-word readers that trim trailing words (probe.retail_words) give a short body; a planted .s built from them shifts every later symbol. Span the corpus extent to `end`, and keep the splat row comments the corpus parser needs, or the plant is refused as uncovered.
