# C0152 — A divide by a constant compiled as a real divide: put the divisor in a local, the second one late
tags: lever,divide,constant,local,reciprocal,trap · date: 2026-10-06 · phase/task: 1.8.1/T10 · origin: mmx6 W10 SLUS 4BB5C

Symptom: retail divides by a constant with a real runtime divide (plus its divide-by-zero trap), where cc1 folds a literal `/ 10` into a reciprocal multiply. The draft is long by the multiply sequence and is misaligned after it.

Lever: hold the divisor in a local assigned the constant (`s32 d = 10; q = v / d;`), one local per block that divides. If one function has two divisors, use two locals. Assign the second local late, just before its use (after any other use of the same constant). Then the freshly reloaded divisor of the trap lands where retail has it.

Case: W10 SLUS_013.95:0x8004BB5C (T10.c8, 56 → 61 then match), rock_04:0x800F2CC4 (T10.c17, two divisors den/den2, 64 → match).
Related: C0110 (remainder spelling), C0135 (statement order).
