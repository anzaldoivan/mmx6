# C0264 — ROCK_X6.DAT slot joins: three readers of D_800E0398, packed records, byte-checked sheet offsets
tags: mmx6,rock_x6.dat,loader,asset-map,redux,vab · date: 2026-10-25 · phase/task: 2.2/T4 · origin: T4.c1

Context: 2.2 T4 asset map (docs/leads/assets.tsv).

- Slot table D_800E0398[247][2] = {lba, bytes}, filled by func_800147D0. Slots 0..0xF2 are the DAT base plus a
  header pair read to 0x80103000. Slots 0xF3..0xF6 are the XA/STR streams, opened by name (D_8006CD42 + i*22).
- Three readers. func_8001642C(slot,dest,..) reads raw. func_80016548(slot,&size) returns the lba (streams).
  func_80014F70(slot,mode,dest) is the packed async read and carries most stage loads.
- The retry path 0x80016178/0x80015EC0 re-enters func_80014F70. Find the callers of all three readers.
- Packed record: u32 n, u32 total, then n x (cmd,size). cmd>>16 picks a handler in D_80010038[4].
- Handler 2 (0x80015A0C) opens a VAB head into bank (cmd>>8)&0xFF.
- The slots a packed parse rejects are exactly the slots func_8001642C reads raw. This cross-checks the static decode.
- Runtime check: tools/mmx6/redux/datloads.lua sets Exec breakpoints on the three readers and logs ra, a0 and a1.
  The site at ra-8 of each hit should already be decoded statically.
- Check a workbook's disc offsets against our disc before trusting them. Compare its bytes column at that offset.
- The Tweaks DAT-Offsets sheet uses another disc layout: 46 of 52 checkable rows did not match.
